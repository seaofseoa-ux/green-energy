Clean energy shouldn't cost a fortune to switch to.

Three proven, budget-friendly ways an average household can go green — starting this year.

Community Solar Gardens

You don't need to own a roof, or thousands of dollars, to benefit from solar power. Community solar gardens let neighbours subscribe to a share of a local solar array and receive a credit on their electricity bill for the power it produces. Subscriptions are often available with no upfront cost, no installation, and no maintenance — making solar accessible to renters, apartment dwellers, and anyone whose roof isn't a good candidate for panels.
